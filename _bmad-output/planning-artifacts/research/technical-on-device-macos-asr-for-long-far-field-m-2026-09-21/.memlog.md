- (event) Pre-research empirical finding (project evidence, not web): WhisperKit 1.1.0 large-v3-turbo drops 11-19% of reference content words per AMI meeting as contiguous >=25-word blocks with zero output text; mechanism in source = SegmentSeeker skips a whole window when noSpeechProb>0.6 and avgLogProb<=-1.0, and the app sets temperatureFallbackCount=0 with no VAD chunking.
- (event) Project evidence (WhisperKit 1.1.0 source + CLI reruns of ES2004a): WhisperKit's own CLI with the app's flags keeps 88% of reference content words and drops no block; the app keeps 71% and drops 19%. The CLI leaves firstTokenLogProbThreshold nil; the app inherits the library default -1.5. TextDecoder ends a window with no text when the first token's logprob is under that threshold and relies on a temperature fallback to retry; the app sets temperatureFallbackCount=0, so the empty window is seeked past. Runs F/G queued to confirm.
