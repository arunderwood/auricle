WAV vs compressed audio

Question: why does auricle store WAV instead of mp3 or m4a?

Outcome

The MVP keeps 16 kHz mono PCM16 WAV. Size is a real but post-MVP concern.

Locked decisions

  • MVP keeps WAV. Retention defaults to delete-sooner and auricle keep is v1.1, so size does not bite yet.
  • "WAV is outdated" is not a reason to change.

Rejected

  • mp3/m4a for the MVP working copy: it solves no MVP problem and adds an encode step to the recovery copy.

Weak points that survived

  • Decision 1.4 calls AAC "non-byte-sliceable". That is wrong; the WAV choice stands on other reasons.
  • Unverified meetings are never auto-deleted, so they grow unbounded at about 115 MB/hr.
  • Post-MVP transcode to mono AAC at about 64 kbps (about 29 MB/hr) is undecided until "listening only vs re-processing" is answered.

Voices

  • 🏗️ Winston, architect: the boring format wins when you control the pipeline.
  • 📊 Mary, analyst: audio is the recovery layer; lossy encoding trades recovery detail for size.
  • Dara (generated storage skeptic): the slicing argument is false, and "acceptable for cache" fails once audio is kept long-term.
